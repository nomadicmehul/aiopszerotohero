# Stage 0 — Orientation & Career Map 🟢

> **Goal:** Understand the AI-native ops landscape, the roles inside it, and plan your personal route through this curriculum.

## Why it matters

From real job postings (full teardowns in [career/job-profiles.md](../../career/job-profiles.md)):

> *"Operate and evolve our central AI infrastructure across all tech squads"* — AI Operations Lead
> *"Move beyond experimentation and build the systems, standards, and feedback loops that allow AI workflows to be evaluated, monitored, trusted, and improved"* — Senior AI Reliability Engineer
> *"Design and maintain the foundational infrastructure that enables our AI Software Engineers to build and scale generative AI applications"* — AI Platform Engineer

Three different companies, three different titles — one skill set. This stage gives you the map.

## The role landscape

| Role title | Center of gravity | Typical seniority |
|---|---|---|
| **AIOps Engineer** (classic sense) | AI/ML applied to IT operations: anomaly detection, log analysis, incident prediction | Mid |
| **AI Platform Engineer** | LLM gateways, model provisioning, self-service infra for AI teams | Mid–Senior |
| **AI Reliability Engineer / AI SRE** | Evals, observability, SLOs, incident response for AI systems | Senior |
| **AI Operations Lead** | Agentic SDLC, org-wide AI governance, enablement, change management | Senior/Staff |
| **AI Infrastructure Engineer** | GPUs, inference serving, Kubernetes for ML/LLM workloads | Mid–Senior |
| **MLOps / LLMOps Engineer** | Model lifecycle: training, deployment, versioning, pipelines | Mid–Senior |

You don't pick one now. Stages 1–6 are common to all of them; Stages 7–9 let you specialize.

## Core topics

1. **The two directions of "AI + Ops"** — *AI for Ops* (AI helping you operate systems) vs *Ops for AI* (operating AI systems). Senior roles demand both.
2. **How AI systems fail differently** — no stack trace: silent degradation, drift, hallucination, inconsistency. This mental model shapes the whole curriculum.
3. **The platform mindset** — enable teams without becoming a gatekeeper: paved roads, guardrails, self-service.
4. **The market** — read 10 live job posts for the titles above and highlight recurring requirements. You'll see this curriculum in them.
