# Certifications — What's Worth It

Honest guidance, community-maintained. Certificates open doors; capstones ([Stage 10](../stages/10-capstones/)) close offers. Spend accordingly.

## Strong signal (worth the money/time)

| Cert | Why | When |
|---|---|---|
| **CKA** — Certified Kubernetes Administrator | The single most-cited cert in AI platform job posts; hands-on exam, real credibility | After Stage 2 |
| **CKS** — Certified Kubernetes Security Specialist | Pairs perfectly with Stage 9; rare + valued | After Stage 9 |
| **HashiCorp Terraform Associate** | Cheap, quick, ubiquitous requirement | After Stage 2 |
| **AWS Solutions Architect Associate** (or Azure AZ-104 / GCP ACE) | The cloud baseline check | After Stage 1–2 |

## Useful secondary signal

| Cert | Why | When |
|---|---|---|
| **KCNA / KCSA** | Cheap CNCF entry certs; nice early momentum | Stage 2 |
| **AWS AI Practitioner** / **Azure AI-102** / **GCP Professional ML Engineer** | Shows managed-AI-stack literacy; AI-102 valued in Azure shops, ML Engineer respected but ML-heavy | Stage 7 |
| **FinOps Certified Practitioner** | Differentiator for the cost-management angle of Stage 5 | Stage 5 |

## Skip (for this track)

- Generic "prompt engineering" certificates — no hiring signal
- Vendor AI badges without hands-on exams — CV filler
- Expensive ML/data-science certs — wrong track; you're operating AI, not training models

## The 80/20

**CKA + one cloud associate + Terraform Associate + flagship capstone repo** beats any longer list of certificates for the roles in [job-profiles.md](job-profiles.md).
