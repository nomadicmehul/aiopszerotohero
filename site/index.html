📦 Learn with any AI agent

This curriculum ships as Agent Skills — an open format (a folder with a SKILL.md) supported by Claude Code and a growing list of agents. Clone the repo, point your agent at it, and it becomes your personal AIOps mentor: it knows every stage, quizzes you, reviews your labs, and tracks your progress locally.

aiops-mentor

Your guide through the curriculum. Assesses where you should start, walks you through each stage, explains concepts, and suggests what's next based on your learning log.

aiops-lab-reviewer

Reviews your hands-on lab work against the stage's skills checklist — like a senior engineer doing code review on your learning projects.

aiops-quiz

Generates spaced-repetition quizzes from any stage you've completed, so the knowledge sticks before you move on.
